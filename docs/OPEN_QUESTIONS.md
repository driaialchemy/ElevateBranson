# Open questions

| Question | Why it matters | Recommended default | Who answers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What is the official program list? | Programs, volunteer choices, and home content depend on it. | Use the public phone menu and media pages as the working list, status verify: Elevate Connections, Elevate Outreach, Elevate Work, Elevate Entrepreneurs, Elevate Kids, Elevate Rides, Elevate Community, Elevate Health, Elevate Coffee (with Neighbors HUB and Cookies by Frank), Elevate Thrift Store. | Who will edit content, and how technical are they? | Chooses the CMS workflow. | One to three non-technical staff. Sanity Studio on the Free plan. Those editors are Administrators, because the Free plan has no Editor role. | Staff lead, when the Sanity project exists |
| Where do donations go today? | Give must not collect card data inside the app unless Apple has approved the nonprofit. | Neon CRM forms, reached from [elevatebranson.org/donate](https://elevatebranson.org/donate/). The app opens that page in the system browser. Assume the org is not approved for in-app fundraising. | Whoever owns the Apple Developer account, only if in-app Apple Pay donations are wanted |
| Is there an online merch store? | Merch is physical goods and must check out outside the app. | No current store URL was found. Merch screen uses a clearly marked placeholder until a URL exists. | Staff, when a shirt store exists |
| How are campus tours booked? | Decides between a deep link and an in-app form. | The public page [elevatebranson.org/schedule-a-tour](https://elevatebranson.org/schedule-a-tour/) is a WPForms form (name, phone, email, requested date, time, details), not a calendar product. Open that page in the in-app browser. Keep an in-app form as the offline fallback. | Staff, to confirm who receives the website form |
| How is the affordable housing list maintained? | Resources must not invent listings. | No public spreadsheet or PDF was found. Staff maintain HousingListing records in the CMS. The list starts empty, with a placeholder empty state. | Staff who keep the housing list |
| What newsletter platform is in use, and does it have a feed? | Push and the newsletter screen need a source that staff already update. | Email newsletter is Constant Contact. The website also publishes issues under [/news](https://elevatebranson.org/news/). WordPress RSS responds at [elevatebranson.org/feed](https://elevatebranson.org/feed/). v1 reads the website. No Constant Contact API. | Communications staff, if a different source should win |
| Are brand assets available? | Theme, logo, and contrast depend on them. | None are in the repo. Use a neutral warm placeholder and a text wordmark. Record the asset request in HUMAN_TASKS.md. | Whoever has the logo, colors, and fonts |
| Do Apple, Google, and Expo accounts exist? | Required to ship and to send push. Not required to build. | Assume they do not exist yet. Build locally. Store submission waits on HUMAN_TASKS.md. | Whoever will own the legal entity accounts |
| Where should volunteer, tour, and prayer forms go? | These messages contain personal data and must reach the right staff inbox. | Volunteer mail goes to the published address Volunteer@ElevateBranson.org. Tour requests use the existing website form first. Prayer uses a placeholder server env var until a real inbox is supplied. Never ship recipient addresses in the app. | Staff, for the prayer inbox and any tour inbox that differs from the website form |
| Which privacy policy covers the app? | Prayer and volunteer data need a notice that matches what the app does. | Link to [elevatebranson.org/privacy-policy](https://elevatebranson.org/privacy-policy/) and add an in-app privacy notice. That page says it covers the website and social channels. | Whoever can update the policy so it covers the app |
